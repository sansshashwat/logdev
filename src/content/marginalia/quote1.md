title: "Not Mine, But Kept / 01"
---

“When Fleabag said, “with all the love I have for her. I don't know where to put it now.” 

And when Anne Carson wrote,
“you remember too much,
my mother said to me recently.
why hold onto all that?
and I said,
where can I put it down?”

And when Mitski sang,
“I don't know what to do without you
I don't know where to put my hands”

And when Donnie Smith in Magnolia (1999) cried,
“I really do have love to give!
I just don't know where to put it!”

And when Emily Dickinson once wrote,
“we outgrow love, like other things
and put it in the drawer -”
