Sebastian Vettel bowed to his Red Bull RB9 after winning the 2013 Indian Grand Prix.

I love this picture. There is something about the way he bows to the car that feels almost tender — like gratitude towards a machine that has carried you through something you could never have done alone.

Vettel named his 2013 RB9 **Hungry Heidi**, after Heidi Klum. He had a habit of giving his cars female names, and this particular one carried him to his fourth consecutive World Drivers' Championship, winning 13 races along the way.

Maybe that's what I like about the picture. For a moment, the machine isn't just a machine. It is something worth thanking.
